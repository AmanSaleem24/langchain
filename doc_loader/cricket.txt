Upon the field where dreams take flight,
The batsmen stand with courage bright.
The bowler runs, the ball takes aim,
And millions cheer the timeless game.

The willow strikes, the ball flies high,
A glorious six adorns the sky.
The fielders leap, the crowd roars loud,
As victory shines upon the proud.

Each wicket tells a tale of skill,
Each run reflects a fighter's will.
Through sunny days and skies of rain,
The players rise and try again.

In Test matches, patience takes its stand,
In ODIs, strategies are planned.
In T20, the boundaries fly,
And hopes soar upward to the sky.

From dusty streets to stadium lights,
Cricket unites us through its fights.
It teaches teamwork, strength, and grace,
And gives each dreamer a rightful place.

Sometimes we win, sometimes we fall,
Yet courage matters most of all.
For beyond the trophies and the fame,
Lies the true spirit of the game.

And when the final stumps descend,
The love of cricket will never end.
Across the world, its legends stay,
As new young dreamers rise to play.
